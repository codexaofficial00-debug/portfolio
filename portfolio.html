Who I Am

  

    

      
I'm Arfa Eamna, a BSCS undergrad at Islamia University of Bahawalpur, RYK Campus, currently in my 7th semester. Over the past few months I've been deep in self-practice, building real ASP.NET MVC projects end-to-end — database, backend, and UI.
